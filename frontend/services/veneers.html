Overview

About our wood varieties

Different jobs call for different timber, and we stock the full range: Pine Wood Baton for battens, frames & lightweight structural work; Meranti Wood for its light weight, stability and clean finish; Sagwan (Teak) Wood and Ivory Coast Wood for furniture, doors and applications where natural durability, water resistance and a rich grain matter most.

Every log is inspected before milling, so what you get is seasoned, straight-grained timber ready for cutting, carpentry or direct use in your project.
