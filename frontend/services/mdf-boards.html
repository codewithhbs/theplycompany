Overview

About our HDHMR & MDF boards

Medium-density fibreboard gives modular furniture makers something plywood can't: a perfectly uniform, edge-machinable core. We stock Action Tesa, Century & Greenply MDF in multiple densities, ideal for CNC-routed profiles, laminate pressing and paint-grade shutters that need a flawless, grain-free surface.

We also stock HDHMR (High Density HMR) boards — a denser, tougher alternative to standard MDF with superior screw-holding and moisture resistance, widely used for shutters, wardrobe frames and high-traffic furniture edges.
