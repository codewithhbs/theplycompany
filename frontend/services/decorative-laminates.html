Overview

About our mica & laminates

Mica is the surface your family touches every day, so consistency between sheets matters as much as the design itself. We stock trusted brands — Merino, CenturyLam, Advance & CountryLam — spanning high-gloss, suede matte and textured wood-grain finishes, pressed under high temperature and pressure for a surface that resists scratches, heat and daily wear.

We also stock matching PVC edge banding tapes for a clean, finished edge on every panel — so your furniture edges look as good as the face.
