Our Story

A family business built on one-stop convenience

From a single timber counter to a complete one-stop solution for all plywood needs across Delhi & NCR — this is how Pradeep Timber Enterprises came to be.

Our Beginning

A business that grew on reputation, not advertising

Pradeep Timber Enterprises started as a timber trading counter run by the Aggarwal family, serving local carpenters who needed material they could trust without inspecting every sheet themselves. Word spread the old way — one satisfied carpenter recommending us to the next.

As Delhi's construction and interiors scene grew, so did our range: Plywood, PVC Boards, HDHMR, MDF, Mica, Adhesives, Hardware, Flexi Ply, Shuttering Ply, and premium woods like Pine Wood Baton, Meranti, Sagwan, Ivory Coast & Teak. Today we're proud to call ourselves a true one-stop solution for every plywood need.

Ankit Aggarwal and Pradeep Aggarwal personally oversee sourcing and quality control, working alongside a team that can also handle chaukhat making, randa on wood, carpentry and door installation — from supply to setup.

Our Mission

To be the single, dependable source for every plywood need — boards, timber, hardware and installation — so clients never have to shop around.

Our Vision

To be the first name Delhi thinks of for timber, laminates and boards — the way a trusted family doctor is the first call in a health scare.

Our Values

Honest grading, fair pricing, and standing behind every sheet we sell — even the ones that leave the yard on credit and a handshake.
